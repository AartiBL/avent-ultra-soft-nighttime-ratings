How to read this dashboard

Trend basis. The charts use successful snapshots from 16, 19 and 20 October 2026. Amazon distributions are source-displayed percentages; Philips provides counts. They are shown separately and never converted or pooled.

Daily change. Amazon.de’s displayed total increased by four on 20 October, but the accessible visible review IDs did not change. Newly observed review text therefore remains zero.

Shared pools. Philips.nl and Philips.de expose the same 306-review pool and are counted once. Amazon.de’s review pool includes other product variants.

Historical review coding. The theme baseline covers 147 usable texts: 140 from the Philips shared pool, five Amazon.nl reviews and two in-scope Amazon.de reviews. Of the 140 Philips texts, 139 were marked incentivized.

Collection limits. bol.com returned HTTP 403. SCF095 remains unavailable until a qualifying listing is verified. Missing data stays unavailable rather than being inferred.
